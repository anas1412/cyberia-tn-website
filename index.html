Our approach

A clear, four-stage engagement. You decide at the end of stage three whether to continue with us.

  1. 1

    Conversation

    You tell us what you're trying to solve. We ask questions until we understand the decision in front of you.

  2. 2

    Assessment

    We study your processes, tools and constraints, and talk to the people who do the work.

  3. 3

    Recommendation

    A written recommendation with options, costs and trade-offs, so you can decide with confidence.

  4. 4

    Delivery Optional

    We build the solution ourselves, or support your team or vendor while they do.

Our work

Selected engagements and the results they delivered.

B2B SaaS

How a B2B SaaS startup cut onboarding time by 80%

Onboarding took 3 weeks of manual demos, outdated docs and repeated support questions. We added a chatbot trained on their documentation, automated daily standup summaries, and AI skills for repetitive support and setup tasks. Onboarding dropped from 3 weeks to 4 days.

E-commerce

From 500 support tickets to near zero

An e-commerce brand was hiring 3 support agents per month just to keep up. We deployed an AI agent handling 90% of inquiries autonomously. Support costs dropped 70%, and the team went back to building product.

Accounting

2 people, 10x output

An accounting firm automated invoice processing, expense reports and bank reconciliations. Two junior accountants delivered the output of a 10-person team. Billable hours per client dropped 60%, and mistakes became rare.
